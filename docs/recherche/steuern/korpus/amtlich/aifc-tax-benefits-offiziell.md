Tax Benefits

The Astana International Financial Centre (AIFC) offers its participants preferential tax regime that combines international standards with long-term guarantees of stability. All AIFC companies are tax residents of Kazakhstan, while enjoying certain benefits.

The AIFC tax regime complies with international standards, including BEPS (Base Erosion and Profit Shifting) – a set of measures aimed at preventing the erosion of the tax base and the shift of profits out of the country – and FATF, the intergovernmental financial monitoring body responsible for developing global standards for combating money laundering and the financing of terrorism, as well as for assessing the compliance of national AML/CFT systems with these standards.
