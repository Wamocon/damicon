01 Overview

Damicon runs a raspberry plantation from the row to the invoice: what is ripe where, who picked it, how fast it was cooled, what it cost and what it earned. The portal is divided into four areas that follow the path of the fruit — Field, Yard, Office, Market.

What this is about

With raspberries, market leadership does not come from volume but from proof. Cooling curve, batch, block of origin, picker and treatment record have to be complete for every delivery. That is exactly what Damicon records.

State of this release

The portal is a prototype. Its main functions work against a real database: sign-in, role permissions, site management, row block locking, picking tasks with photo evidence, document storage. Two modules are still a menu entry without a view. The figures show baseline values until the first fully measured season is available. Without a database connection the portal runs in demo mode on sample data.

Each area has its own landing page showing its modules as tiles. Which of them are visible depends on the role of the account signed in.

02 Using the portal

This chapter describes the interface: how to sign in, how the navigation is built and where the settings are. What the individual modules do is covered in the next chapter.

2.1 Signing in

  1. Open the portal. Without a language code in the address you are taken to the German version.
  2. On the start page choose “Open portal”, or go to the sign-in page directly.
  3. Enter your email address and password.
  4. If a second factor is set up for the account, the six-digit code is requested next. Jumping straight to the overview does not help — the check sits in front of the page, not inside it.
  5. After signing in you land on the overview with the four areas and the farm's key figures.
Accounts for trying it out

For the prototype there is one account per role: admin@damicon.demo, ceo@damicon.demo, leitung@damicon.demo, buchhaltung@damicon.demo, brigade@damicon.demo, pfluecker@damicon.demo, erzeuger@damicon.demo, kunde@damicon.demo.

All eight accounts share the same password: DamiconDemo2026! — If the portal runs without a database, sign-in is skipped entirely and it works on sample data. Every view states at the top right which source it is reading from.

2.2 How the interface is built

On a desktop the sidebar carries the navigation, on a phone the bar along the bottom. Both lead to the same pages.

Sidebar

On the left edge, from tablet width upwards. The overview at the top, below it the four areas as collapsible groups with their modules. An area in which the role may not see a single module does not appear at all. Only administration sees a last group below them, “Administration”, with the pages AI providers, Rate limit and Knowledge base. The sidebar can be collapsed to a narrow icon column; the toggle for that sits in the header. At the bottom are the handbook, the person signed in, the security page and sign-out.

Header

Carries the breadcrumb of the open page, the toggle for the sidebar width, the search, the language selector, the colour scheme and — for administrators — the “View as” field. The bell sits on the far right: a click opens the notifications at the right edge of the screen, on the phone as a sheet from below, and as long as there are none, it says so there. Alerts and deadlines are still shown in the modules themselves. B2B clients and pickers do not see the bell. On the phone it keeps the way back, the search and the notifications.

Search

Opens with a click on the magnifier in the header to the left of the bell, and at the desk also with the keys / or Ctrl+K (Mac: ⌘K). The search window opens at the top centre of the screen. The search finds the overview, the zones, the modules and the pages Security, Compliance report and Handbook, and only what the role also sees in the navigation. It searches names in the selected language. Below them, under “Mentioned in”, it lists the modules whose description contains the term, with the passage. Arrow keys choose a result, Enter opens it, Esc closes the search. With an empty field it lists the pages you opened yourself most recently. If nothing matches, it offers to ask the AI about the term. It does not yet find row blocks, batches, pickers or documents.

Bottom bar (phone)

Below tablet width it replaces the sidebar. The menu works in two levels: first the overview and the four areas, then, after a tap on an area, its modules, with the area itself as the first entry. The arrow at the top left leads back to the list of areas. A tree with all 27 entries at once is the right shape for a standing column, not for a surface you open with your thumb; one level at a time is. The bar stays operable while a sheet is open, so a tap on the account button takes you straight on to language, colour scheme, handbook, security and sign-out.

Overview

The landing page after signing in. A greeting, then the four areas with their key figures. Management and administrators additionally see “Today's essentials”: maturity, one sentence from the latest audit report, the changes since the previous one, and five tiles for audit, tax, legal, risk and finance.

Area page

Each area has its own page showing its modules as tiles with a short description. It is the route to the modules on a phone, where there is no sidebar.

Module page

Shows the module's view: tables, forms, figures. The top right states whether the data comes from the database or is sample data. An empty list usually shows the module's icon. Where entries can be created there and the role is allowed to, a button is added that jumps to the matching form, opens it and puts the cursor in the first field. After saving, the button briefly shows a green check mark. Android phones vibrate at that moment, with a different pattern on an error; on the iPhone the tap on the button itself ticks. Modules that have not been built yet state in one sentence what they will do.

List with detail view

Modules with many entries show a list with filters and pages, starting with the picking tasks. Clicking an entry opens the detail view with tabs on the right, and the list stays usable next to it. The list becomes narrower so that the detail view gets the room and its content fits without scrolling where possible. Where there is too little room, for example on a phone or next to the open AI chat, the detail view takes the place of the list. Filters, page and the selected entry are part of the address: a link shows exactly this view, and Back closes the detail view again. New entries are created with the button directly above the list.

2.3 Switching language

The language selector sits in the header, and on a phone in the account sheet. Four languages are available: German, English, Kazakh and Russian. German and English are translated in full, Kazakh and Russian largely so for the prototype; where a translation is missing, the German text appears. The language is part of the address, so a given language version can be linked to. This handbook follows the language the portal is currently running in and has its own selector at the top right.

2.4 Colour scheme

Light and dark, switched in the header. The choice is stored in the browser and applies to this handbook as well. Without an explicit choice the portal follows the operating system setting.

2.5 Changing role

The effective role is that of the account signed in; it is shown at the bottom of the sidebar. To see a different role, sign in with the matching account. Administrators additionally have the “View as” field: it changes only what appears on screen. Write permissions continue to follow the profile — and they do so in the database, where switching something in the interface has no effect.

2.6 Working without a connection

Out in the field the network is unreliable. Reports on picking tasks, crates, working hours and cooling measurements therefore go into a queue on the device and are sent later on their own. They pass through the same checks as an entry made at a desk — a report from the queue carries no more permissions than the person who created it.

04 How-to guides

The workflows that cover the core of the operation. The paths refer to the sidebar.

4.1 Applying and lifting a pre-harvest interval lock

After a crop protection treatment a row block must not be harvested until the pre-harvest interval has elapsed. The portal applies this lock itself.

  1. Sign in as farm management and open Field → Row blocks and status.
  2. At the bottom, “Record treatment”: choose the row block and the product. The interval comes from the product catalogue, it is not typed in by hand.
  3. The block is immediately set to locked. The “Lock” column shows product, treatment date, interval, release date and the days remaining.
  4. A status change is now impossible. The rule lives in the database and applies even if someone works around the interface.
  5. Once the interval has elapsed, a “Release” button appears. It lifts the lock and signs off the treatment at the same time.
  6. Clicking the status tiles filters the table; the filtered state can be passed on as a link.

4.2 Reading the risk of a row block

The “Risk” column combines five factors into a score from 0 to 100: missed picking round, waiting-period block, rejects, cold chain and recording gap. It always names the main reason too, never just a number.

  1. Open Field → Row blocks. Every block carries a level (low, medium, high), the score “x of 100” and the main reason. A block without a shortcoming reads “no shortcoming”.
  2. Level high means at least two serious factors, or an open task that falls due before the waiting-period block ends. A single shortcoming gives “medium” at most.
  3. “How the risk score arises” below the table names the rule, the weight and the current limits for each factor.
  4. Administration, farm management and field crews see the rating. Roles that do not see all inputs (rotation plan, treatments, cold chains) get no score rather than one that is too low.
  5. Administration and CEO maintain weights, limits and thresholds under Office → Settings, section “Block risk: weights and thresholds”. The portal rejects values where the threshold for “high” is not above the one for “medium”, or where all weights would be zero. Every change appears in the history.
  6. Every hour the portal checks the blocks with level high and reports them in the bell.

4.3 A picking task with photo evidence

  1. Open Field → Picking tasks with photo evidence. The list shows all tasks, latest due date first, 20 per page. The pills above the list filter by status and show the number of matches. Search, brigade and period sit next to them, on a phone behind “Filters”.
  2. Expand “New picking task” above the list and enter row block, brigade, due date with time (farm time, Almaty) and target quantity. Locked blocks are not offered for selection and are rejected by the database as well. After saving, the new task opens in the detail view.
  3. Clicking a task opens the detail view on the right; on a phone it replaces the list. In the Overview tab, “Next step” says what to do next. The arrows at the top go to the previous and next task, Esc or Back closes the view.
  4. As the brigade, accept the task, start picking and upload photo evidence in the Photo evidence tab. On a phone this opens the camera directly. The file goes into a private storage area and is only ever shown through short-lived signed links.
  5. “Report quantity” moves the task to evidence review. From then on its due date no longer counts: only tasks the brigade is still picking can be overdue.
  6. Farm management or administrators then see thumbnails of the evidence in the overview, enter the quality factor and release the task.

4.4 Finding a record (data search)

The search in the header finds not only pages and modules but also records: crates, batches, picking tasks, pickers, row blocks, documents and customers.

  1. Tap the search button to the left of the bell. The search dialog opens at the top centre.
  2. Enter at least two characters, for example a batch number or part of a name. “Pages and modules” lists the pages, “Search in data” starts the search in the records.
  3. Hits name the type (crate, batch, picking task, picker, row block, document, customer), the code and the context. The arrow keys select a hit, Enter opens it, Esc closes the search.
  4. The search shows only what your role may read anyway: a customer finds no pickers, a producer no customers. It requires a login and returns at most 50 hits. A “%” or “_” in the input counts as a character, not a wildcard.

4.5 Showing the chain of evidence for a delivery

This is the procedure that matters to the customer: from the punnet back to the person who filled it.

  1. Open Field → Picking tasks with photo evidence and select a task.
  2. In the “Chain of evidence” tab of the detail view, the batch is shown: cooling curve with the 60-minute limit, quantity and rejects, the crates with the person who filled them, and the residue record.
  3. “Record crate” assigns a crate to a person. Only then does the chain reach from the customer all the way to the picker.
  4. “Report working time” supplies the denominator for picking performance in kilograms per hour.
  5. “Record cooling measurement”: the database derives minutes and verdict from the time of picking. Above 60 minutes the interface reports a breach and the goods have to be downgraded.
  6. On the overview the figures then show the changed actual value — calculated, not entered.

4.6 Filing a document

  1. As an office role, open Office → Document management.
  2. Under “File document” enter title, category, reference and date. The file itself — PDF or image — is optional.
  3. Stored files open from the “File” column through a signed link. There is no public direct access.

4.7 Setting up access: invitation and second factor

New customer accounts are created by invitation. Each person sets up their own second factor.

  1. As an administrator or farm management, open Office → Roles and permissions.
  2. Under “Invitations” enter the email address and role. This sits here because it is the same matter as the permission matrix above it — only its writing side.
  3. The invited person opens the invitation link and sets their own password. That page is reachable without signing in: whoever lands there does not have an account yet.
  4. An open invitation can be withdrawn as long as it has not been redeemed.
  5. For the second factor: open “Security” at the bottom of the sidebar, or the same entry in the account sheet on a phone.
  6. “Start” shows a QR code and a key — both are handed out only once. Scan it in an authenticator app, enter the six-digit code and confirm. Only then does the factor take effect.

4.8 Upload, approve and delete a document in the knowledge base (administration)

The knowledge base holds the law, tax, compliance, audit and risk texts from which the assistant takes its references. Only administration can add documents here, and every new document must be approved by a second person (four-eyes principle).

  1. Sign in as administration and open the page “Knowledge base” under “Administration” in the sidebar. The “Administration” group is the last one below the zones and is visible to administration only. The page shows “Upload documents and books” on top and the list “Existing documents” below it. The list and the upload listing can be switched between “List” (one row per document, details expandable) and “Grid” (large tiles with all details); the browser remembers the choice. The list shows cluster, source type and level for each document and can be filtered by cluster. Material from before typing appears as “Not classified”.
  2. Under “Upload documents and books” choose one or more files (PDF, Markdown or text, any length, no size limit). The browser reads the files itself. A PDF must contain text, a pure scan is blocked.
  3. Choose the cluster. Title (from the file name), area (Law, Tax, Compliance, Audit or Risk), source type and text basis are suggested by the analysis for each file; every entry can be changed. The source type follows where the text comes from: statute, case law, specialist literature, practice paper, internet source, forum and more. The cluster is the way the text reached us: Books, Publications or Internet source. The two are independent: a law from a government site is a legal norm from the internet. Only internet source, forum and internet research always come from the web. The portal sets the level of the source from the source type.
  4. Not every source type is equally reliable. Blogs, forums, experience reports, review portals, Wikipedia and internet searches count as unreliable internet sources: the assistant uses them only when there is no solid source for a question, and then says right at the start that there is no official government source. The link to the source is required. Specialist literature and official sources take priority.
  5. Choose which office roles may see the documents (administration is always included) and press “Upload documents”. The text goes to the server in packages and is split and embedded. Progress and remaining time are shown above the list.
  6. The document now appears in the list as “Awaiting approval” and is not searchable for anyone yet. The person who uploaded it cannot approve it themselves.
  7. A second person with administration rights opens the list, chooses “Review” and reads the start of the text, source type, level and link. “Approve” makes the document searchable for the chosen roles, “Reject” blocks it.
  8. Internet sources, forums, reference works and similar sources expire after 12 months (practice papers and internal documents after 24). The list then shows “Review due” and search no longer finds the source until a second person checks it again and extends it.
  9. The same file cannot be uploaded twice. The original file is not kept, only the text. Books as PDF are an exception: their original is available for review until approval or rejection and is deleted afterwards.
  10. To delete, choose “Delete” on an uploaded document and check title, area and number of sections in the window. Deleting cannot be undone. Documents loaded by script have no delete button.
  11. For every source the assistant names the source type, the level and the date. It labels hints from the internet, forums or AI texts as unchecked and never bases a binding statement on them alone. If there are only hints, it says that the knowledge base holds no reliable source.
  12. Material loaded before typing appears as “Not classified” in the list. Above the list the section “Classify existing documents” opens: for each document the page suggests a value from the link, the previous level and the legal reference, with a reason and a confidence. Type and cluster can be changed per document. Tick the documents with a complete choice and choose “Review classification”. The window shows which documents will no longer be found in their area, only count as a hint, or change their level. Only “Save classification” changes anything, and every classification is logged. Sources that are already classified are never touched by this route.
  13. Books and long documents (for example PDF after text recognition, OCR) go in through the same section “Upload documents and books”. Choose only the cluster (books, publications or internet source) and the roles there, then select several files at once. There is no limit on file size or page count: the browser reads the file itself and sends the text in packages. The PDF is also saved as an original (in chunks, resuming after a lost connection, with a progress display) while the book waits for approval. Up to 1 GB per file is possible, and all waiting originals together may take up to 50 GB: if that is exceeded, please upload separately, one part first and approve it, then the next. In the review window “Open original” shows it in a new tab, and for books uploaded earlier “Add original” adds it afterwards (the file is compared with the stored text). After approval or rejection the original is deleted. If the page sequence check found missing pages, the review window lists them by number. The second person checks the original and enters a note, for example “Blank pages in the book”. Without a note the book cannot be approved, and the bulk approval skips such books. A PDF needs a text layer, a pure image scan yields no text.
  14. Before uploading, the page shows for each file the number of characters and an assessment of the text quality (readable, check, poor) with the reasons, such as chopped words or many special characters. A text rated “poor” is not uploaded, the file should be created again with OCR. The title is taken from the file name and can be changed. “Upload books” starts the series, an overall display shows book, packages, percent and the estimated time left, and a bar per book shows its progress (please keep the page open), and “Cancel” removes the book that was started.
  15. When reading a PDF the page also checks the page sequence: it reads the printed page number at the edge of every page and compares it with the order in the file. If the number jumps (for example from 47 to 50), pages are missing in the scan. The book then shows which printed pages are missing and after which PDF page, and can only be uploaded if you explicitly confirm the gaps. It is better to rescan the pages and create the file again. For laws the page also shows missing article numbers (repealed articles may be among them). The information goes with the book to the second person: during the review the note “Pages are missing in the scan” appears, and the book counts as “check text” at best. The second person checks the original and enters in the review window the printed page numbers that are correctly missing (for example blank or note pages). If the entry does not cover all gaps, real content is missing and the book is not approved. In that case the missing pages are loaded as a PDF with text recognition in the review window (the system reads the printed page numbers, inserts the pages at the position of the gap and offers the book for approval again), or the book is deleted and the whole PDF is uploaded again. Pages confirmed as correctly missing do not lower the quality of the book, open gaps do.
  16. Each document in the list of existing documents shows its quality in percent, for example "Quality 95%". The value says how well the stored text can be read (text recognition, chopped words, character noise; tables of contents and indexes are not counted). For a book with pages missing from the scan the value drops further. From 90 percent the text is good, from 75 percent it should be checked, below that it is poor. Newly uploaded books get the value automatically; for older documents the button "Calculate quality" computes it. Before uploading, the page already shows the value for each file.
  17. Under “Check and clean up the holdings” the page checks the whole knowledge base at the push of a button: research working documents (orders, indexes, plans), documents explicitly marked as outdated, duplicates, documents whose content is already in an uploaded book, due review dates and documents without a usable link. The content comparison runs in steps with a progress display and can be switched off. Every finding comes with its reason, and for duplicates and documents covered by books “Show comparison” displays the passages word for word, because similarity alone does not prove the same statement. The check changes nothing. Documents are only removed once you have selected them, entered a reason, confirmed and typed the number of documents. Every removal is logged, is final and is not copied back by the synchronisation to production.
  18. If the connection breaks off, the book appears as “Incomplete” in the list. It cannot be approved, only deleted and uploaded again. Like every document, a book stays unchecked until a second person approves it.
  19. The page helps with the review through an assessment: every waiting document carries the recommendation “approve”, “review first” or “reject”, with the reasons (incomplete, poorly readable, not classified, internet source without link, very short, last resort only). The assessment only checks mechanical things and does not replace reading the preview, which shows the start, middle and end of the text.
  20. Many documents can be decided in one step: above the list “documents are waiting for your review” appears. Select all or only the recommended ones or tick individual documents, then choose “Approve selection” or “Reject selection”. The window names every document with its recommendation, and after confirming it shows what happened to each document. Every document is checked and logged individually, and the bulk review does not approve your own uploads either.
  21. Approved documents first exist in the preview environment. After the change is merged, a workflow copies the approved documents of the knowledge base into production, and only those: no users, no operating data. Unchecked and rejected documents stay in the preview.
  22. You do not set area, source type and text basis yourself: the analysis suggests them for each book, from the content (structure, technical terms, notes on translation) and, if you want, additionally with the AI. For each book three selection fields appear with the suggestion, a statement of confidence (“Confident suggestion”, “Check suggestion”, “Uncertain, please look”) and the reason. Every value can be changed, including the title. What you changed yourself is no longer overwritten by the AI. The AI analysis sends an excerpt of about 6,000 characters per file and the file name to the configured AI provider; the tick “Analyse with AI” turns it off, then only the rules apply.
  23. The classification can be changed at any time later: in the list choose “Change classification” for a document, then change cluster, area, source type and text basis. The window shows which level follows from the source type and how the source is used in the area. The change is logged. For an approved upload, area, source type and text basis are not changed by the person who uploaded it but by a second person (four-eyes principle, also enforced in the database); the cluster can always be changed.
  24. The AI assistant helps with managing the knowledge base (administration only): it gives an overview of holdings and distribution, names what is waiting for review or is not classified, analyses a document and explains whether the classification is right. It also checks the holdings for outdated material, duplicates and documents already contained in a book, shows the comparison in the original wording on request and gives the quality of a document in percent. It proposes a change of classification and the removal of documents as a card that you confirm. It cannot upload files or approve documents, that stays with you and the second person.
  25. The list can be filtered: at the top by cluster, below by search term in the title, area, reliability (official, reliable, indicative only, unreliable, not classified), source type, status (for example waiting for approval), origin (uploaded or existing holdings) and the role a document is visible to. The filters apply together, and the number behind each entry shows the hits. Unreliable sources are unverified internet sources that the assistant names only as a last resort and labelled. The bulk review only acts on the documents shown.

4.9 Documenting a delivery with ЭСФ (document register)

Since 1 January 2026 every delivery must be documented through the state ЭСФ system. The portal does not issue the ЭСФ. It keeps the register, prepares the details and shows which delivery has no document yet.

  1. As administration, CEO or accounting open Yard → Delivery note (ЭСФ). “Deliveries without ЭСФ” lists all delivered shipments without an issued document, the oldest first. The age counts from the delivery day. The deadline is set under Office → Settings (default 15 calendar days after the delivery day, Tax Code art. 493) and can be changed there. Deliveries past the deadline are marked.
  2. “Create draft” stores a snapshot of the details: buyer with identification number, variety, quantity, price per the price list on the delivery day and, if the farm was VAT-registered on that day, the VAT. What is missing (price, variety, identification number, VAT rate) is listed under “The draft still lacks”. Whether the price list holds net or gross prices is set on the price list. The ЭСФ always calculates net plus rate; for a gross list the draft splits the total. Check the draft with accounting before issuing.
  3. Issue the ЭСФ in the state portal with these details. Then record it in the register with “Record as issued”: enter the ЭСФ number and date. From here on the draft no longer changes.
  4. If the ЭСФ is confirmed, “Record confirmation”. If it is rejected, “Record rejection” with a reason, then “Start a new draft”.
  5. Paper is only permitted during an outage of the state system. “Keep on paper” therefore requires a reason. Confirmed documents and documents kept on paper are closed.
  6. Farm management can see the register but records nothing. The database rejects any status change the process does not know. Every action is logged. The automatic handover to the state system follows once the authority grants access.

4.10 Reporting seasonal contracts to ЕСУТД (reporting process)

The conclusion, change and termination of every seasonal contract belong in the state unified system ЕСУТД. The portal does not report by itself. It shows what is to be reported and records what has been reported and what proves it.

  1. As administration, CEO or farm management open Office → Integrations.
  2. Under “Create a new conclusion” enter the person, the start of the contract and optionally the contract number. For the conclusion the portal calculates the deadline: five working days after both parties have signed (without a date: from the start of the contract), without a holiday calendar. Once it has passed, “Deadline passed” appears on the row and in the risk radar.
  3. Enter changes and terminations under “Create a change or termination”: contract, type, date and, for a change, what changes. A change must be reported within 15 calendar days of its date, a termination within three working days of its end. The days are set under Office → Settings.
  4. “Open reports as CSV” downloads the details (name, ID, type, date, contract number) as a plain table for entry in the state system. There is no official import format. The file contains personal data: delete it after entry. Every download is logged.
  5. After reporting in the state system choose “Record as reported”: report date (not in the future), optionally a confirmation number and a proof. Upload the proof first in the evidence register (Office → Documents, category ЕСУТД proof), then select it here.
  6. A wrongly recorded report can be reopened: date, confirmation and proof are cleared. A report that is still open can be removed. In the personnel view the ЕСУТД field of a person shows “open” as long as one of their reports is open. Accounting can see the list but records nothing.

4.11 Keeping a proof with a validity period

Certificates, permits and contracts come to an end. The evidence register calculates the status from the period, it is not set by hand.

  1. Open Office → Documents. When adding a document, or through “Edit”, enter valid from, valid until (or “Valid without end date”), the advance warning in days (1 to 365, default 60), the person responsible, the legal basis and, where relevant, the partner.
  2. The status is valid, expiring (within the warning period), expired or open. Open means: no period recorded or not yet in force. The portal does not invent dates, a proof without a period stands out as a gap.
  3. “Valid until” is the last day of validity, on the following day the proof counts as expired. The day changes at midnight in Almaty.
  4. The header counts the states. Proofs that are expiring or expired also appear in the risk radar of the compliance cockpit.
  5. When a certificate is renewed, open “New version” instead of overwriting the old document. The fields are prefilled; enter period and file of the new version. The old version stays under the name as an “earlier version”, but no longer counts as current and no longer raises an alarm in the risk radar.

4.12 Grants: keeping the checklist of a dossier

The portal does not connect to a grant portal. It keeps a checklist for every dossier and reminds you of deadlines. What an application needs is set by the funding body; the list is not an official form.

  1. Open Office → Grants. Under “Dossier checklists” each dossier shows its progress (“x of y done”), the number of open mandatory items and the number of missed deadlines.
  2. For a dossier without items choose “Adopt general suggestion”: five items (application form, legal form and identification number, cost and investment statement with quotes, proof of land, payment receipts). The suggestion is not an official catalogue: compare it with the funding body's requirements and adapt it.
  3. “Add item”: description, an optional deadline, and whether it is mandatory. “Done” ticks an item off, “Reopen” undoes that, “Remove” deletes it.
  4. A dossier counts as complete when all mandatory items are done. Items past their deadline count as “Deadline passed”.

4.13 Importing the file of a temperature logger

A temperature logger in the vehicle records the temperature of the trip. The portal takes over its file instead of the values being typed in by hand.

  1. Open Yard → Collection rounds and unfold “Import logger file” at the planned delivery.
  2. Choose the logger’s file, CSV or JSON. CSV: time and temperature per line, separator comma, semicolon or tab, decimal comma or decimal point, up to 2 MB. Optionally enter a serial number or vehicle as identifier. JSON: a list with time and temperature per entry, the time as text or as Unix time.
  3. Times without a time zone use the zone chosen under “Logger clock” (default Almaty, also UTC, Berlin and Moscow). Values of the last 24 hours are taken over. Older rows, rows in the future, duplicates and unreadable rows are left out, and the portal gives the numbers after the import.
  4. Each value is rated like a manual measurement (up to 4 degrees fine, up to 8 degrees warning, above that violation) and carries the origin “logger”. A delivered or cancelled delivery no longer accepts values.
  5. The portal also reads other notations: files in Windows-1251 and Windows-1252, times like 14.10.26 08:15 or 8:15 PM, Excel time values in the time column, temperatures in Kelvin or Fahrenheit (per column header, converted to degrees Celsius), a time zone in the column header (“Time (UTC+6)”) and the JSON format of elproCLOUD (fields value, timeStamp in UTC and unit).

4.14 Maintain the business case of the rollout

The page calculates from items that are recorded. What is not recorded stays open instead of appearing as zero.

  1. Open Office → Business case. On an empty page choose "Take over model values" to start with the values of the metric list of 23 September 2026: investment and recurring cost as evidenced, the annual benefit as estimated.
  2. Under "Items of the calculation" open "Record an item": type (investment one-off, operation per year, benefit per year), category, description, amount, currency (euro, tenge, ruble), date and whether the amount is evidenced or estimated. Every line can be changed and removed.
  3. Under "Assumptions" set the discount rate, the exchange rate with date and source, the full cost per kg, the annual quantity and the first month of live operation. The annual quantity comes from the deliveries as soon as they cover a full year.
  4. Under "Plan and actual" record the cost that was actually avoided per month. It is set against the plan and shown as points on the curves.
  5. "Save the current state" freezes the calculation with items, assumptions and exchange rate. A snapshot never changes again, a later rate does not affect it. "As document (PDF)" outputs the calculation in the Damicon layout.
  6. Administration, CEO and accounting can record. Operations management sees the page.
  7. The section “Loss benefit from operating data” derives the benefit from the loss rate: decline against the baseline times annual volume times full cost per kilogram. “Adopt as benefit item” creates the item (origin estimated) and updates it on a repeated click. With fewer than ten batches or no improvement the derivation stays open.
  8. The calculation rate has a benchmark (default 20 percent) and two comparison rates (16 and 24 percent), which the page shows as a rate check next to the net present value. All three values are set under Office → Settings.

4.15 Drive a pickup round at neighbouring farms

A vehicle collects produce at neighbouring farms and brings it to the cold store. From the start of picking, the 60-minute cold chain rule counts.

  1. Open Yard → Collection rounds. The office plans under "Plan a pickup round" the date, the vehicle, the neighbouring farms and the expected quantity. With an address the system orders the stops by the shortest route, without one they go to the end.
  2. The driver opens the round and chooses "Start round". Only then can pickups be recorded.
  3. At each stop "Record pickup": the collected quantity and the neighbouring farm's answer to how many minutes ago picking began. If it is missing, the pickup counts as a warning. A stop that does not take place is recorded with "Cancelled".
  4. When all stops are done, the driver reports the arrival at the cold store. Up to 45 minutes from the start of picking the pickup is fine, up to 60 minutes a warning, beyond that a violation.
  5. Completed rounds no longer change. "Pickup order (PDF)" outputs the order for the driver and, after completion, the record, both in the Damicon layout.
  6. The vehicle capacity is a default under Office → Settings and can be overridden per round. If a round plans more than the payload, the planning warns. Each stop can carry a collection window (from, to). The estimated arrival per stop uses the travel time and the time per stop (setting), and a window that the arrival misses is marked.

4.16 Connect an integration to a partner system

Events such as an issued ЭСФ invoice can be delivered as a signed webhook to a partner system, for example to accounting. Without explicit activation no record leaves the system.

  1. As administration open Office → Integrations and find the section "Delivery to partner systems" at the bottom.
  2. At the integration open "Configure": type "webhook", the https address of the partner and the name of the environment variable that holds the shared secret, for example DAMICON_HOOK_ESF. The secret itself is never in the portal, it is set in the server environment.
  3. Tick "Delivery active" and save. If the variable is missing on the server, the portal says so and nothing is delivered.
  4. The partner system checks the signature in the header X-Damicon-Signatur (HMAC-SHA256 over timestamp and content) and recognises repetitions by the Idempotency-Key.
  5. The queue shows every event with its status. A temporary error is retried after 1, 5, 15, 60 and 240 minutes, after that the entry is "failed". "Try again" and "Deliver now" are reserved for administration and the CEO.
  6. For a partner system with its own format, administration stores a template with placeholders under “Target format” that maps the envelope (for example 1C via OData). Credentials for basic authentication come, like the secret, from an environment variable of the server, never from the portal.
  7. The partner can confirm receipt through the portal's feedback address (with signature and timestamp). The entry then reads “confirmed”. The operator sets up a regular call of the delivery through the workflow integrationen-ausliefern.

4.17 Assigning roles

The role of an account determines what it may see and do. Only administration can assign it.

  1. As administration open Office → Roles and permissions. Under “Accounts and roles” every account is listed with its role.
  2. Choose the new role in the row and “Save”. The change applies from the next page the account opens.
  3. Your own role cannot be changed here. Customer accounts are created through invitations and keep their role.
  4. The last administrator keeps the role: the database rejects the withdrawal, even with direct access. Every change is logged with the old and the new role.

4.18 Creating and blocking staff accounts, changing the password

Administration creates accounts for staff. Whoever leaves the farm is blocked rather than deleted: the name stays visible in logs and histories, but the account has no rights any more.

  1. As administration open Office → Roles and permissions. Under “Create staff account” enter name, e-mail address and role and choose “Create account”. The portal shows the temporary password once: pass it on securely, it appears nowhere afterwards.
  2. The person logs in and sets their own password on the Security page (account menu, “Change password”). It needs at least 12 characters. Not allowed are your own e-mail address and passwords of fewer than four different characters.
  3. To block, open “Block account” in the account's row, enter a reason (3 to 300 characters) and choose “Block”. A blocked account has no role at once and cannot log in. The row shows date and reason.
  4. “Unblock” restores the account with its role. Your own account and the last active administrator cannot be blocked, the database rejects both.
  5. Creating, blocking and unblocking appear in the log.

4.19 Maintaining deadlines, capacities and benchmarks in the settings

Business values are not in the program but in the settings: deadlines for ЭСФ and ЕСУТД, vehicle capacity, benchmarks of the investment calculation and the weights of the block-risk rating. Access rights are not part of it, roles and the database govern those.

  1. Open Office → Settings. The values are grouped by area: tax, labour law, collection rounds, finance, delivery, security and block risk.
  2. Each setting names description, unit, default value and source with date. Administration and CEO may change them; for tax and labour law the responsible specialist role (accounting, farm management) may too. Whoever may not change a value still sees it.
  3. Enter the new value, optionally a reason, and choose “Save”. The portal checks type and limits. An empty field means “not set”: then no check applies, instead of a value being invented.
  4. The history below shows who changed which value when, from what to what, with the reason. The history cannot be changed.

4.20 Messages on a pre-order and the delivery schedule

Agreements on quantity and date stay with the pre-order instead of vanishing in phone calls and messengers.

  1. Open Market → B2B portal. Under “Messages on pre-orders” every pre-order has its thread. The customer sees only their own, the office sees all.
  2. Unfold a pre-order, write the message (up to 2000 characters) and “Send”. The database sets author, role and time from the login.
  3. Sent messages cannot be changed or deleted. A cancelled pre-order gets no new messages.
  4. “Delivery schedule as calendar file” downloads the upcoming delivery dates as an ICS file for the calendar. It is a download tied to the session, not a subscription link: download again to refresh. The customer gets only their own dates.
  5. For data protection cases there is “Anonymise” (administration, CEO): text and name of the message are removed, the history stays. A reason is mandatory and appears in the log.

4.21 Playing, uploading and replacing training videos

The portal plays the short onboarding videos in the person's language where the video exists in that language. Administration and farm management may upload.

  1. Open Market → Training. Under “Training videos” each video shows title, topic, duration and, if mandatory, the mark “Required”.
  2. Play the video in the window. It loads only when played, which spares the mobile network. If there are several languages, the buttons below the video switch the language. If yours is missing, the portal takes Russian, then Kazakh, German and English.
  3. To upload, use “Upload or replace video file” below the video: choose language and file (MP4 or WebM, up to 500 MB) and “Upload”. The upload runs in chunks and can be resumed after an interruption. An existing file in the same language is replaced.
  4. “Remove file” deletes one language version.

4.22 The bell: reading notifications

The bell in the header collects what needs attention, without anyone opening every module. It is visible to administration, CEO, farm management, accounting, field crews and producers.

  1. The number on the bell shows the unread notifications. Tapping the bell opens the list.
  2. Every hour the portal creates notifications about expired and soon expiring proofs (to the responsible person, otherwise to administration and farm management), about ЭСФ past the deadline (accounting, administration), about overdue ЕСУТД reports (farm management, accounting, administration) and about row blocks with risk level high (farm management, administration). The CEO receives what administration receives.
  3. A state reports only once: if “expires soon” later becomes “expired”, a new notification arrives. For ЭСФ, ЕСУТД and block risk there is one summary per day.
  4. Tapping a notification marks it as read and opens the module. “Mark all as read” tidies the list. Read notifications disappear after 90 days.
  5. The prerequisite is that a schedule calls the generation (workflow benachrichtigungen-erzeugen). Without it the bell stays empty. The operator sets this up.

Voice modeA live conversation with the assistant without a visible chat: Himbi leads the conversation: large in the middle while listening, at the left edge while explaining on wide screens, sized to fit the screen. Running text is off by default and can be switched on with the subtitles button in the bar; the browser remembers the choice (on a phone Himbi stays small, without running text and beside the highlighted area, so the page stays visible), with lips moving in time with the voice while speaking (the small figure in the corner is hidden meanwhile; if Himbi is switched off in the settings, a coloured circle shows whether it is listening, thinking or speaking), and the assistant can jump to and highlight a section on its own. Interrupt as in a conversation: just start talking, or tap Himbi. To start: the “Talk” button in the header, or in the chat the send button while the input field is empty. Voice mode can do the same as the chat, including entering data. Before any change is saved, it shows the change clearly outlined on screen, and you approve it by saying “Yes” or decline it with “No”. While it explains, a frame outlines exactly the spot it is talking about, and it expands collapsed sections for that. It also opens the zones Field, Yard, Office and Market and the compliance audit report, filtered by audit, tax, law or risk on request. Say “Stop”, “Wait”, “Hold on” or “No” to halt Himbi while he is speaking or thinking. The conversation stays open, and Himbi knows how far you listened. Whatever you say right after that is the next question, for example “Stop, show me the complaints instead”. If you keep talking while he is still thinking or has only just started to answer, he adds your words to the question instead of answering only the first part. Noises and a short “mhm”, “yes” or “right” do not interrupt him: he briefly gets quieter and then carries on. He only stops when you say something with content. To end voice mode, say “End voice mode” or “Bye Himbi”. So that this always works, the microphone listens throughout the whole conversation, also while the assistant is speaking; it recognises its own voice and does not react to it. The voice always follows the language of the text, not the interface language: a Russian sentence sounds Russian even when the interface is German, both when reading answers aloud in the chat and in a conversation. This also applies to very short answers such as “Да.”. While an approval is open, the status reads “Waiting for your yes or no”. The approval only appears after Himbi has spoken the sentence before it, for example “I am now clicking Create”. “Yes, please”, “Do it”, “Да, конечно” or “Әрине” also count as consent, and an “uh” before it does no harm. A counter-question such as “Yes?” approves nothing. If you ask a new question instead, Himbi declines the open action and then answers your question. A command only takes effect when your utterance starts with it: “How do I close the task?” does not end voice mode. On a slow connection Himbi speaks in slightly lower audio quality so that the voice does not stutter.

Four-eyes principleAn uploaded knowledge document is searchable only after a second person has approved it. The uploader does not approve. The database enforces this, not just the interface.
Source typeThe kind of source of a knowledge document (statute, case law, specialist literature, internet source, forum and more). It determines the level, what the source is good for in which area, and when it must be reviewed again.
Day companionHimbi helps you organise the working day. He only suggests an action as a question, and even after your “yes” nothing is saved until you approve it. Once a day Himbi starts on his own: in voice mode he asks about the day's situation right at the start (the question then appears in the chat history like any other), otherwise he asks in a speech bubble whether he should tell you what is urgent today.
